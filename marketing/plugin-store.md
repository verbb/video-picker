Video Picker gives authors a visual field for finding and selecting hosted video. Connect supported provider sources, browse account collections or paste a URL, and hand templates useful video data instead of an opaque string.

Browse the connected source, search beyond the current collection where the provider allows it, or paste a known video URL. Thumbnails and metadata help authors confirm they have the right item before saving the field.

## Features

- **Hosted video providers:** Work with Bunny Stream, Cloudflare Stream, Dailymotion, Mux, Sprout Video, Vimeo, Wistia and YouTube.
- **Visual discovery:** Search and confirm a result with its title and thumbnail.
- **URL selection:** Paste a known provider URL when browsing is unnecessary.
- **Field controls:** Decide whether authors can paste URLs, search public videos or browse the configured sources, and enforce duration limits where supported.
- **Embed defaults:** Configure options such as autoplay, muted playback, looping and controls for the field.
- **GraphQL:** Expose selected video values to headless front ends.
- **Layered caching:** Reuse provider sources, video metadata, and resolved records.
- **Element cards:** Show selected videos in supported Craft element cards.
- **Explorer and preview controls:** Choose whether each field shows the video browser and preview.
- **Custom sources:** Register another provider behind the picker’s shared interface.
- **Embed helpers:** Render a selected video with its embed HTML or use the embed URL directly.
- **Multiple video sources:** Configure multiple provider accounts for different channels or clients, then decide which sources are available to each field. Developers can register another source type or extend existing behaviour through events.
